Allowance vs. Direct Write-Off

Castle Company · 20X1

Castle Company provides estimates for its uncollectible accounts. The Allowance for Doubtful Accounts had a credit balance of $17,280 at the beginning of 20X1 and a $22,410 credit balance at the end of 20X1 (after adjusting entries). If the direct write-off method had been used to account for uncollectible accounts (bad debt expense equals actual write-offs), the income statement for 20X1 would have included bad debt expense of $17,100 and revenue of $2,200 from the collection of previously written-off bad debts.

Required: Determine bad debt expense for 20X1 according to the allowance method.

Solution

Direct write-off expense = actual write-offs17,100 of accounts were written off: a debit to the allowance

Revenue from collecting written-off debts = recoveries2,200 reinstated: a credit to the allowance

Balance before adjustment17,280 − 17,100 + 2,200 = 2,380 credit

Filler #: the adjusting entry22,410 − 2,380 = 20,030

Bad debt expense for 20X1, allowance method20,030
