Prairie Rose Inc.: Interest Capitalization

On December 31, 20X3, Prairie Rose Inc. borrowed $2,000,000 at 9% payable annually to finance the construction of a new building. In 20X4, the company made the following expenditures related to this building: February 1, $960,000; May 1, $1,200,000; September 1, $1,500,000; December 31, $900,000.

Additional information:

  1. Other debt outstanding:
    • 10-year, 8% bond, dated December 31, 20X0, interest payable annually: $3,000,000
    • 5-year, 10% note, dated December 31, 20X2, interest payable annually: $2,000,000
  2. Interest revenue earned in 20X4: $35,000.

Required

  1. (a) Determine the amount of interest to be capitalized in 20X4 in relation to the construction of the building.
  2. (b) Prepare the journal entry to record the capitalization of interest and the recognition of interest expense, if any, at December 31, 20X4.

Note 1: Capitalization period = months from the date of the expenditure to December 31, 20X4, divided by 12.

Note 2: The December 31 expenditure is made on the last day of 20X4, so its capitalization period is 0/12.

Note 3: Weighted-average accumulated expenditures of $2,180,000 exceed the $2,000,000 construction loan by $180,000. Avoidable interest on the $180,000 excess is computed at the weighted-average interest rate on other debt.

Weighted-average interest rate on other debt
= Total interest on other debtTotal principal of other debt

= 8.8%
Avoidable interest
= Construction loan × Construction loan rate + (Weighted-average accumulated expenditures − Construction loan) × Weighted-average interest rate on other debt
= $2,000,000 × 9% + ($2,180,000 − $2,000,000) × 8.8%
= $180,000 + $180,000 × 8.8%
= $180,000 + $15,840
= $195,840
Actual interest
= Interest on construction loan + Interest on bond + Interest on note
= $2,000,000 × 9% + $3,000,000 × 8% + $2,000,000 × 10%
= $180,000 + $240,000 + $200,000
= $620,000
Note 4: Interest revenue of $35,000 earned in 20X4 is reported as revenue. It is not subtracted from actual interest.
Interest capitalized
= Lesser of avoidable interest and actual interest
= Lesser of $195,840 and $620,000
= $195,840
