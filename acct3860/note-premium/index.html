Wayward Hunting Supply · fiscal year ends December 31

On January 1, 20X1, Wayward Hunting Supply lends cash to Turner Bait & Tackle and receives a $100,000, five-year note with a 10% stated interest rate. Interest is received each December 31, and the note is due December 31, 20X5. The market rate for notes of similar risk is 8%. Wayward records the note at face value with a separate premium account.
