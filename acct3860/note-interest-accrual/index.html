Interest Accrual on a Note

Esquire Company · 6% note · June 30, 20X1 to March 31, 20X2

On June 30, 20X1, the Esquire Company sold some merchandise to a customer for $30,000. In payment, Esquire agreed to accept a 6% note requiring the payment of interest and principal on March 31, 20X2. The 6% rate is appropriate in this situation.* All rates are annual unless stated otherwise!

Required:

  1. Prepare journal entries to record the sale of merchandise (omit any entry that might be required for the cost of the goods sold), the December 31, 20X1 interest accrual, and the March 31, 20X2 collection.
  2. If the December 31 adjusting entry for the interest accrual is not prepared, by how much will income before income taxes be over- or understated in 20X1 and 20X2?
