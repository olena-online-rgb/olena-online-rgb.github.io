Novak Family Hardware · Pontiac, Illinois · fiscal year ends December 31

On January 1, 20X1, Novak Family Hardware lends cash to Prairie Lumber Co. and receives a $120,000, five-year note with a 5% stated interest rate. Interest is received each December 31, and the note is due December 31, 20X5. The market rate for notes of similar risk is 7%. Novak records the note at face value with a separate discount account.

Required: compute the cash Novak lends, prepare an effective-interest amortization schedule, and record the entries from January 1, 20X1 through collection of the note.
