Build the Income Statement: Discontinued Operations

Sammy Enterprises · Multiple-step format · Exam practice
Scenario: the Morningstar Division
During 20XX, Sammy Enterprises decided to discontinue its Morningstar Division, a component of the company whose disposal represents a strategic shift in Sammy's operations. Before year-end, Sammy sold the division's assets. The division's results are reported separately, below income from continuing operations, as discontinued operations, net of tax. You have two pieces of information:
  1. The Morningstar Division's operations produced a loss of $150,000 before tax.
  2. Selling the division's assets produced a gain of $60,000 before tax.
Tax rate: 27% on all items. Weighted-average common shares outstanding: 70,000.
Everything is in one bank: the heading lines, section headings, accounts, and subtotal lines, all mixed together. Drag the three heading lines into the heading area in order. Then build the body from top to bottom, putting each section heading, account, and subtotal line where it belongs. Accounts indent once they are placed. Type the income tax expense on continuing operations. Use the scenario to report each discontinued item net of tax: the tax effect goes in the small box and the net amount in the right-hand box. Subtotals fill in as soon as everything above them is right. Finish with earnings per share. A correct amount locks in the moment you enter it (press Enter or move to the next box) and is formatted the way it would appear on the statement. One heading line and one account do not belong on an income statement: they go in the box at the bottom (the heading line can also stay in the bank).
No mouse? Click a tile to select it, then click a zone to add it at the end, or click a placed tile to put the selected one just above it. Press Check my statement whenever you like: ✗ means it does not belong there, ↕ means right place but out of order, and tiles change color when they are right.
