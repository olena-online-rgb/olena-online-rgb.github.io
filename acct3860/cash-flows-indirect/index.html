Indirect Method: Add or Deduct?

Sammy Enterprises · Operating activities · Exam practice
The indirect method starts with net income and adjusts it to the cash that operating activities actually produced. One item at a time, decide: is it added to net income or deducted from it? A right answer moves the item into its place on the statement. A wrong answer sends the item back, and it returns a few turns later. The increases and decreases change every round, so you can play again without memorizing. Not graded.
