Allowance Method: Percent of Receivables

Moondoor Games · fiscal year ends December 31

Moondoor Games sells LARP costumes, foam swords and game kits on account. It uses the allowance method and estimates that 5% of ending accounts receivable will prove uncollectible. On January 1, 20X1, Accounts Receivable had a $120,000 debit balance and the Allowance for Doubtful Accounts had a $6,000 credit balance. During 20X1:

  1. Sales on account, $850,000.
  2. Collections from customers on account, $815,000.
  3. Accounts written off as uncollectible, $7,500.
  4. A $1,200 account written off earlier in 20X1 was recovered: the customer paid in full.
  5. On December 31, 20X1, the allowance was adjusted to 5% of ending accounts receivable.

Required: record the entries, post them to the T-accounts, and present net accounts receivable on the December 31, 20X1 balance sheet.
