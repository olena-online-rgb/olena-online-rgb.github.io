Step 1: Set up the statement Step 2: Sort the cash flows
Step 1. Sammy Enterprises reports operating activities using the direct method: cash received and cash paid, by source. Drag the three heading lines into the heading area in order. Then drag the section headings and total lines into the body in the order they appear on the statement, including the three lines that carry cash from the beginning of the year to the end. One heading tile does not belong on this statement: leave it in the bank. You can also click a tile to select it, then click a zone to place it. Press Check structure when you are done: ✗ means it does not belong there, ↕ means right place but out of order, and tiles change color when they are right. To practice only the sorting, press Skip to Step 2.
