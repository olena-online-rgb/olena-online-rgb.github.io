Cedar Valley and Ironwood: Exchange of Equipment

Example 10. DO ON YOUR OWN. Cedar Valley Company exchanged equipment used in its manufacturing operations plus $2,000 in cash for similar equipment used in the operations of Ironwood Company. The following information pertains to the exchange.

ItemCedar Valley Co.Ironwood Co.
Equipment (cost)$28,000$28,000
Accumulated depreciation19,00010,000
Fair value of equipment13,50015,500
Cash given up2,000

Instructions

  1. (a) Prepare the journal entries to record the exchange on the books of both companies. Assume that the exchange lacks commercial substance.
  2. (b) Prepare the journal entries to record the exchange on the books of both companies. Assume that the exchange has commercial substance.

Matching colors show where a number comes from and where it is used.

Fair value of assets given up
= Fair value of equipment given + Cash paid
= $13,500 + $2,000
= $15,500
Book value of assets given up
= (Cost − Accumulated depreciation) + Cash paid
= ($28,000 − $19,000) + $2,000
= $11,000
Gain or loss
= Fair value of assets given up − Book value of assets given up
= $15,500 − $11,000
= $4,500 gain
Cash paid as a share of the fair value of the exchange
= Cash paidFair value of the exchange
= $2,000$15,500
= 0.13 < 0.25
Note 1: Cash is less than 25% of the fair value of the exchange, so the exchange is nonmonetary.
Gain recognized
= $0
Note 2: When the exchange lacks commercial substance and cash is paid, no gain is recognized.
Initial value of the new equipment
= Fair value of the exchange − Deferred gain
= $15,500 − $4,500
= $11,000
