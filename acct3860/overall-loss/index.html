Long-Term Contract with Overall Loss

On July 1, 20X1, Overrun Builders Inc. contracted to build an office building for Fixed Price Corp. Overrun Builders estimated that the building would take between 2 and 3 years to complete, and on December 31, 20X3, it was deemed substantially completed. Compute the profit or loss to recognize in 20X1, 20X2 and 20X3 under (a) the percentage-of-completion method and (b) the completed-contract method. Ignore income taxes.
