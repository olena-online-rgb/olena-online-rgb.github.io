Aging of Receivables

Zuo Software · 12/31/20X1

Zuo Software categorizes its accounts receivable into four age groups for purposes of estimating its Allowance for Doubtful Accounts.

  • Accounts not yet due = $400,000; estimated uncollectible = 8%.
  • Accounts 1–30 days past due = $50,000; estimated uncollectible = 15%.
  • Accounts 31–90 days past due = $40,000; estimated uncollectible = 30%.
  • Accounts more than 90 days past due = $30,000; estimated uncollectible = 50%.

At 12/31/20X1, before recording any adjustments, Zuo has a credit balance of $22,000 in its Allowance for Doubtful Accounts.

Required:

  1. Estimate the appropriate 12/31/20X1 balance for Zuo’s Allowance for Doubtful Accounts.
  2. What journal entry should Zuo record to adjust its Allowance for Doubtful Accounts?
  3. Calculate Zuo’s 12/31/20X1 net accounts receivable balance.

Bonus question: Assume a customer with A/R balance of $15,000 got bankrupt, and the balance is deemed uncollectible. What is the NRV of A/R after the write off?
