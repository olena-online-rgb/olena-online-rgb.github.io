Causwell Company: FIFO and LIFO from Average Cost

Example 8. DO ON YOUR OWN. Causwell Company began 20X1 with 10,000 units of inventory on hand. The cost of each unit was $5.00. During 20X1 an additional 30,000 units were purchased at a single unit cost, and 20,000 units remained on hand at the end of 20X1 (20,000 units therefore were sold during 20X1). Causwell uses a periodic inventory system. Cost of goods sold for 20X1, applying the average cost method, is $115,000. The company is interested in determining what cost of goods sold would have been if the FIFO or LIFO methods were used.

Required:

  1. 1. Determine the cost of goods sold for 20X1 using the FIFO method. [Hint: Determine the cost per unit of 20X1 purchases.] (Do not round intermediate calculations.)
  2. 2. Determine the cost of goods sold for 20X1 using the LIFO method. (Do not round intermediate calculations.)

Matching colors show where a number comes from and where it is used.Used in this step

Work setup

Units available for sale
= Units in beginning inventory + Units purchased
= 10,000 + 30,000 = 40,000 units
Average cost per unit
Cost of goods sold = Units sold × Average cost per unit
Average cost per unit = Cost of goods sold ÷ Units sold
= $115,00020,000 units = $5.75
Cost of goods available for sale
Average cost per unit = Cost of goods available for sale ÷ Units available for sale
Cost of goods available for sale = Units available for sale × Average cost per unit
= 40,000 units × $5.75 = $230,000
Cost of 20X1 purchases
Cost of goods available for sale = Beginning inventory + Purchases
Purchases = Cost of goods available for sale − Beginning inventory
= $230,000 − 10,000 units × $5.00
= $230,000 − $50,000 = $180,000
Cost per unit of 20X1 purchases
Purchases = Units purchased × Cost per unit
Cost per unit = Purchases ÷ Units purchased
= $180,00030,000 units = $6.00
Note: This is the hint in requirement 1. Each unit bought in 20X1 cost $6.00; each unit in beginning inventory cost $5.00.
Cost of goods sold
= Units sold, oldest layers first
= 10,000 units × $5.00 + 10,000 units × $6.00
= $50,000 + $60,000 = $110,000
Note: FIFO sells all 10,000 units of beginning inventory first. 20,000 − 10,000 = 10,000 units sold come from the 20X1 purchases.
Or, ending inventory first
Ending inventory = Units in ending inventory × Cost per unit of 20X1 purchases
= 20,000 units × $6.00 = $120,000
Note: Since 20,000 units < 30,000 units purchased in 20X1, all 20,000 units left at year-end come from the 20X1 purchases.
Cost of goods sold = Cost of goods available for sale − Ending inventory
= $230,000 − $120,000 = $110,000
Cost of goods sold
= Units sold, newest layers first
= 20,000 units × $6.00 = $120,000
Note: Since 20,000 units sold < 30,000 units purchased in 20X1, all 20,000 units sold come from the 20X1 purchases.
Or, ending inventory first
= Units in ending inventory, oldest layers first
= 10,000 units × $5.00 + 10,000 units × $6.00
= $50,000 + $60,000 = $110,000
Note: LIFO leaves the oldest units: all 10,000 units of beginning inventory, then 20,000 − 10,000 = 10,000 units from the 20X1 purchases.
Cost of goods sold = Cost of goods available for sale − Ending inventory
= $230,000 − $110,000 = $120,000
The three methods side by side
FIFO $110,000 < Average $115,000 < LIFO $120,000
Note: The unit cost rose during 20X1, from $5.00 to $6.00. LIFO sends the newest, higher costs to cost of goods sold, so its cost of goods sold is the highest; FIFO sends the oldest, lower costs, so its cost of goods sold is the lowest.

Cost per unit, average cost method
= Cost of goods available for sale ÷ Units available for sale
= 50,000 + 30,000 × X40,000
Cost of goods sold, average cost method
= Units sold × Cost per unit
20,000 × 50,000 + 30,000 × X40,000 = 115,000
Note: 20,000 ÷ 40,000 = ½, so the 20,000 and the 40,000 become a 2 under the line.
50,000 + 30,000 × X2 = 115,000
Solve for X
50,000 + 30,000 × X = 115,000 × 2
30,000 × X = 230,000 − 50,000
X = 180,000 ÷ 30,000 = $6.00
Cost of 20X1 purchases
= 30,000 units × $6.00 = $180,000
(1) FIFO cost of goods sold
= 10,000 × $5.00 + 10,000 × $6.00 = $110,000
(2) LIFO cost of goods sold
= 20,000 × $6.00 = $120,000
