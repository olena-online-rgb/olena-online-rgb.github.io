Dean Incorporated: Roof Replacement and Change in Estimate

Example 14. DO ON YOUR OWN. Dean Incorporated constructed a building at a cost of $2,400,000 and occupied it beginning in January 1999. It was estimated at that time that its life would be 40 years, with no salvage value.

In January 2019, a new roof was installed at a cost of $300,000, and it was estimated then that the building would have a useful life of 25 years from that date. The cost of the old roof was $180,000. The company accounts for improvements using substitution.

Required:

  1. (a) What amount of depreciation should have been charged annually from the years 1999 to 2018? (Assume straight-line depreciation.)
  2. (b) What entry should be made in 2019 to record the replacement of the roof?
  3. (c) Prepare the entry in January 2019 to record the revision in the estimated life of the building, if necessary.
  4. (d) What amount of depreciation should be charged for the year 2019?

Matching colors show where a number comes from and where it is used.

Annual depreciation, 1999 to 2018
= Cost − Salvage valueService life
= $2,400,000 − $040
= $2,400,00040
= $60,000
Years of depreciation, 1999 to 2018
= Last year − First year + 1
= 2018 − 1999 + 1
= 20
Accumulated depreciation, January 1, 2019
= Annual depreciation × Years of depreciation, 1999 to 2018
= $60,000 × 20
= $1,200,000
Note 1: Accumulated depreciation on January 1, 2019 is used in (d).
Accumulated depreciation on the old roof
= Cost of the old roof × Years usedService life
= $180,000 × 2040
= $180,000 × 12
= $90,000
Note 1: The old roof is part of the building's $2,400,000 cost, so it was depreciated with the building for 20 of its 40 years.
Loss on disposal of the old roof
= Cost of the old roof − Accumulated depreciation on the old roof
= $180,000 − $90,000
= $90,000
Note 2: Nothing is received for the old roof, so its remaining book value is a loss.
