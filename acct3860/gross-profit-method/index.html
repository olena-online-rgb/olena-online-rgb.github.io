Lebanon Supply Company: Gross Profit Method

Lebanon Supply Company uses the gross profit method to estimate ending inventory and cost of goods sold when preparing monthly financial statements required by its bank. Inventory on hand at the end of October was $58,500. The following information for the month of November was available from company records:

Purchases$110,000
Freight-in3,000
Sales180,000
Sales returns5,000
Purchases returns4,000

In addition, the controller is aware of $8,000 of inventory that was stolen during November from one of the company’s warehouses.

Required:

  1. 1. Calculate the estimated inventory at the end of November, assuming a gross profit ratio of 40%.
  2. 2. Calculate the estimated inventory at the end of November, assuming a markup on cost of 60%.

Matching colors show where a number comes from and where it is used.

Work setup

Cost of goods available for sale
= Beginning inventory + Purchases − Purchases returns + Freight-in
= $58,500 + $110,000 − $4,000 + $3,000 = $167,500
Net sales
= Sales − Sales returns
= $180,000 − $5,000 = $175,000
Estimated ending inventory, before the theft
= Cost of goods available for sale − Net sales × (1 − Gross profit ratio)
= $167,500 − $175,000 × (1 − 0.40)
= $167,500 − $105,000 = $62,500
Note 1: Net sales × (1 − Gross profit ratio) is the estimated cost of goods sold: $175,000 × 0.60 = $105,000.
Estimated ending inventory
= Estimated ending inventory before the theft − Stolen inventory
= $62,500 − $8,000 = $54,500
Note 2: The gross profit method estimates the inventory that would be on hand without the theft. The $8,000 stolen during November is not on hand at the end of November, so it is subtracted.
Gross profit ratio
= Markup on cost1 + Markup on cost
= 0.601 + 0.60
= 0.375
Estimated ending inventory, before the theft
= Cost of goods available for sale − Net sales × (1 − Gross profit ratio)
= $167,500 − $175,000 × (1 − 0.375)
= $167,500 − $109,375 = $58,125
Note 1: Net sales × (1 − Gross profit ratio) is the estimated cost of goods sold: $175,000 × 0.625 = $109,375.
Estimated ending inventory
= Estimated ending inventory before the theft − Stolen inventory
= $58,125 − $8,000 = $50,125
