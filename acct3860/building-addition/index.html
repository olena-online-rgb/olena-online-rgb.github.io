Sammy Enterprises: Depreciation of a Building Addition

Sammy Enterprises purchased a five-story office building on January 1, 20X1, at a cost of $5,000,000. The building has a residual value of $200,000 and a 30-year life. The straight-line depreciation method is used. On June 30, 20X3, construction of a sixth floor was completed at a cost of $1,650,000.

Required:
Calculate the depreciation on the building and building addition for 20X3 and 20X4 assuming that the addition did not change the life or residual value of the building. (Hint: Calculate depreciation for the building and addition separately. Assume the addition has no residual value and an estimated life equal to the remaining useful life of the building.)

Matching colors show where a number comes from and where it is used.

= 30 − 2.5
= 27.5 years
Note 1: The hint sets the useful life of the addition equal to the remaining useful life of the building and sets its residual value at zero, so the full cost of the addition is depreciated.
